# Colors and color palettes

Each callout type can have separate colors for light and dark mode. Choose a preset, pick a custom color, or save a palette to reuse across several callouts.

## Light and dark colors, automatically

Each callout has a **Light color** and a **Dark color**. Callout Studio follows Obsidian's current appearance, so your callouts switch automatically when the vault changes between light and dark mode.

## Choosing a color

When you open the color picker for a callout type, you'll find ready-made presets to choose from:

- **Obsidian's original callout palette:** the colors used by Obsidian's built-in callouts.
- **Extra presets:** additional colors included with Callout Studio.

Pick any preset and it's applied instantly to the callout type you're editing.

## Building your own custom palettes

If you want a color scheme you can reuse across multiple callout types, create your own palette from **Settings → Saved color palettes**. Once saved, your palette appears in the colour list on any callout, right alongside the built-in presets. That list can be typed into: click the field and start typing to narrow it by name.

The **Saved color palettes** heading shows the total number of palettes and can collapse the list. Click it, or focus it with `Tab` and press `Enter` or `Space`. Callout Studio remembers whether the section is open when you reopen settings or reload the plugin. If you save more than twenty palettes, **Load more** reveals the rest in one step. On desktop and iPad, the heading stays at the top of the settings pane while you scroll through a long list; on iPhone, it scrolls normally.

On a computer, a new palette opens with the cursor in **Name**. Reopening a saved palette leaves the current focus unchanged. On a phone or tablet, tap the field when you want to type.

You can build a palette in two ways:

### Simple mode

In **Simple** mode, choose one base color. Callout Studio creates matching light and dark backgrounds and accents, then adjusts the contrast to keep the text readable.

### Advanced mode

In **Advanced** mode, you fine-tune all four colors by hand:

- Light accent
- Dark accent
- Light background
- Dark background

As you adjust each one, live contrast warnings let you know if a combination would be hard to read, so you can catch readability issues before you save.

## Palettes and your existing callouts are independent

When you apply a saved palette, its colors are copied to the callout. Editing or deleting the palette later does not change callouts that already use those colors.
